# NPL Regulatory Profile Research

**Status:** Source-backed implementation research, not legal advice or a compliance certification. Each profile needs local bank/compliance approval before activation.

## Ghana — build first

Bank of Ghana's 2025 NPL measures require robust credit-risk governance, Board oversight, reduction-plan action and controlled treatment of restructuring, write-offs and wilful defaulters. UCH should provide: a Board-plan workflow, facility-level evidence, approval gates, monthly outcomes and a regulator-ready evidence pack.

- [BoG NPL measures](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/NOTICE-NO.-BG-GOV-SEC-2025-23-REGULATORY-MEASURES-TO-REDUCE-NON-PERFORMING-LOANS-IN-BANKS-SDIs-AND-NBFIs.pdf)
- [BoG explanatory notes](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Explanatory-Notes-Notice-on-High-NPLs.pdf)

## Nigeria — draft profile

CBN's published prudential guidelines require continuous portfolio review, classify a facility as non-performing at 90 days overdue (including specified roll-over/rescheduling cases), and require a demonstrated period before reclassification after restructuring. The profile must capture classification, repayment capacity, collateral, provisioning and CRMS reconciliation evidence.

- [CBN prudential guidelines](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/OUT/2010/PUBLICATIONS/BSD/PRUDENTIAL%20GUIDELINES%2030%20JUNE%202010%20FINAL%20%20_3_.PDF)
- [CBN CRMS credit-risk objectives](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/supervision/crms.html)

## Kenya — draft profile

CBK prudential guidance defines NPL conditions around 90 days for scheduled facilities and requires reporting of gross NPLs, interest in suspense, provisions and net NPL exposure. The profile must retain those values separately rather than treating a balance as a single NPL number.

- [CBK prudential guidelines](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/PRUDENTIAL-GUIDELINES.pdf)

## UMOA / BCEAO — regional draft profile

The UMOA prudential framework is regional and requires country-specific institution mapping. BCEAO material distinguishes Basel-style 90-day default from the DEC 2000 accounting classifications; UCH must therefore retain source classification and a mapped regulatory classification with evidence, not overwrite one with the other.

- [BCEAO treatment of debit accounts](https://www.bceao.int/fr/documents/quel-traitement-accorder-aux-comptes-debiteurs-sans-mouvements-crediteurs-depuis-une)
- [BCEAO classification distinction](https://www.bceao.int/fr/documents/comment-definit-la-categorie-des-impayes-par-rapport-au-dec-2000)

## South Africa — research required before rule activation

The Prudential Authority supervises governance, risk-management, internal-control, information and accounting systems under its statutory framework. A detailed impairments/NPL rule profile needs an applicable PA standard and the adopting bank's IFRS 9 policy before UCH can activate thresholds.

- [Prudential Authority functions](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/Prudentialregulation/functions-of-the-prudential-authority)

## Required UCH profile fields

Every country profile must version: source publication, effective date, institution type, NPL trigger, classification stages, restructuring/reclassification rule, provisioning fields, reporting calendar, approval requirements, evidence requirements and a named local owner.
