## First Four LinkedIn Posts

### Post 1 — Launch

African banks do not need another disconnected dashboard. They need a controlled path from risk signal to accountable action and evidence.

Universal Credit Hub brings together credit intelligence, NPL early warning, collateral evidence, consent controls, fiscal-data insight and a bank-approved management diagnostic.

Start with a fictional, hands-on product tour: https://universalcredithub.com/demo

No bank data is used in the demo. No production actions are enabled.

### Post 2 — NPL and IFRS 9

NPL improvement is not only a collections problem. It depends on early warning, clean facility data, accountable ownership, collateral evidence and a governed IFRS 9 policy process.

UCH helps a bank test those dependencies in a controlled management diagnostic before committing to a wider programme.

Learn more: https://universalcredithub.com/forensics

### Post 3 — Read-only diagnostic

Before asking a bank for an integration, ask a better question: what can be established from approved files, evidence and accountable owners?

UCH’s management diagnostic is designed as a read-only, bank-approved fact-finding exercise. The bank validates the finding, severity, scope and next step before any pilot proceeds.

Request a discussion: https://universalcredithub.com/contact-sales

### Post 4 — Consent and evidence

Sensitive bank workflows need more than data access. They need a clear purpose, consent where required, role-based control and an audit record that shows who did what and why.

That is the operating principle behind Universal Credit Hub’s cross-workspace design.

Explore the platform: https://universalcredithub.com/
