# UCH Differentiated Product Research — 30 July 2026

## Evidence-led conclusion

UCH should not claim to be a global first or a replacement for a core-banking system. Established platforms already cover broad lending lifecycle, decisioning and automation. UCH can be distinctive by making the **bank's governed operating loop** tangible: one operating event becomes prioritised work for named people, a human approval point, auditable evidence and a management outcome across credit risk, customer operations, fraud, collateral and reporting.

## What the market already proves

| Market evidence | UCH implication |
|---|---|
| Temenos presents end-to-end origination, servicing, collections, compliance and data-driven decisioning. | Do not position UCH as another generic lifecycle suite. Integrate with a bank's system of record and own the cross-control risk/evidence layer. |
| nCino's current positioning combines AI assistants with role-based, governed, auditable bank workflows. | AI in UCH must create a recommendation and evidence trail, never a silent lending, reversal or provision decision. |
| Bank of Ghana's current concentration-risk, ICAAP and NPL measures focus on resilience, governance, stress, data and accountable management action. | Start every Ghana bank conversation with a governed control loop and measurable pilot rather than a model claim. |

## Product thesis: UCH Bank Operating Loop

1. **Sense** — ingest a bank-approved extract or event and show data completeness, country policy and confidence.
2. **Prioritise** — turn risk into a named, role-specific work item with materiality, SLA and dependency.
3. **Decide** — show the recommendation, rules/evidence used, permitted action and the human decision owner.
4. **Prove** — retain the rationale, approvals, attachments and immutable timeline in an examiner-ready evidence pack.
5. **Improve** — show baseline, progress and accepted outcomes to management without claiming autonomous remediation.

## Build sequence

1. Public demo: a synthetic **Banker Shift** that lets a CEO, CRO or Operations leader start a macro, credit or payments event and watch UCH route work across desks.
2. Authenticated product: role-relevant `Today` work queue, with linked NPL, fraud, consent/collateral and evidence cases.
3. Ghana pilot: approved data contracts, model/policy configuration, maker-checker approvals, historical back-test and evidence export.
4. Country packs: retain the common operating loop but configure country policy, regulator evidence, language and residency per bank.

## Product truth boundary

The public demo remains fictional and browser-only. A real bank workflow requires the bank's authority, configured roles, approved policy thresholds, data-processing terms, integration tests and bank validation of outcomes.
