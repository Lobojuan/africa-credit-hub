# Forbidden Claims

Do not use these claims in GTM copy, AI drafts, sales scripts, or website text without explicit review.

- Do not claim UCH guarantees loan approvals, lower defaults, regulatory approval, or credit outcomes.
- Do not claim UCH replaces legal counsel, regulators, licensed credit bureaus, or mandatory compliance review.
- Do not claim autonomous AI calls are live or legally cleared.
- Do not imply recipient data was obtained without permission or provenance.
- Do not imply UCH makes adverse credit decisions on behalf of institutions unless that exact workflow is built, licensed, and reviewed.
- Do not send first outreach without human approval in the MVP.
